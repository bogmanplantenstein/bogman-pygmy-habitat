# Sources and attribution

Habitat descriptions in this dataset are paraphrased summaries written for Bogman Plantenstein, not copies of the sources below. Each species in the widget links its own sources.

## Soil, habitat and range
- Nunn, R. & Lambers, H. (2017). The impact of soil type on Pygmy *Drosera* distribution. *Carnivorous Plant Newsletter* 46(1): 13–19. https://cpn.carnivorousplants.org/articles/CPNv46n1p13_19.pdf
- International Carnivorous Plant Society. *Guide to the Pygmy Drosera* (descriptions after Lowrie, Nunn, Robinson, Bourke & Fleischmann 2017, *Drosera of the World*). https://carnivorousplants.org/cp/taxonomy/pygmyDrosera
- Ng, B. *Fierce Flora* species profiles. https://www.fierceflora.com/
- Western Australian Herbarium. *FloraBase*. https://florabase.dbca.wa.gov.au/
- Lowrie, A. & Conran, J.G. (2014). *Drosera micra*. *Carnivorous Plants of Australia Magnum Opus*, Appendix: 1272. Type specimen label (Kew K001392875; PERTH 08989001).
- Robinson, A.S., Cross, A.T., Meisterl, M. & Fleischmann, A. (2018). *Drosera albonotata*. *Phytotaxa* 346(3): 221–236. https://doi.org/10.11646/phytotaxa.346.3.2
- Krueger, T.A. & Fleischmann, A. (2020). When three become two: *Drosera coalara* links *Drosera citrina* with *Drosera nivea*. *Carnivorous Plant Newsletter* 49(1): 6–16.

## Cultivation reports (summer dormancy flag)
- Terraforums grower threads: "Pygmy sundews that have a summer dormancy", "Pygmies dormant now", "Pygmy summer survival tips", "Pygmy Drosera aestivation". Anecdotal reports.

## Climate
- Rainfall, monthly temperatures and dry months: long-term station averages, *Climate statistics for Australian locations*. © Commonwealth of Australia, Bureau of Meteorology. http://www.bom.gov.au/climate/averages/ (27 stations; site numbers in build/stations.py.)
- Hot-extreme temperature: Bogman Climate Map, from data obtained from the NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project, funded through the NASA Earth Science/Applied Science Program. https://power.larc.nasa.gov/
- Species locations: research-grade iNaturalist observations (https://www.inaturalist.org), used only to place species within the climate grid. No observation coordinates are published here.
