Sources & method
  • Soil type: Nunn & Lambers (2017), Carnivorous Plant Newsletter 46(1).
  • Habitat & range: ICPS Guide to the Pygmy Drosera, after Lowrie et al. (2017); Fierce Flora (Boaz Ng) field profiles; FloraBase; the D. micra type specimen label (Lowrie & Conran 2014); Robinson et al. (2018) for D. albonotata.
  • Typical hot extreme: Bogman Climate Map, the hottest month's 90th-percentile daily high from NASA POWER data. Grid cells are ~50 km, so treat values as ±3°C.
  • Moisture band: scored from each species' published habitat descriptions. A guide, not a measurement.
  • Rainfall, monthly highs and lows: Australian Bureau of Meteorology long-term averages from 27 stations, weighted to each species' records.
  • Dry months: months where average rainfall (mm) is less than twice the average temperature (°C), the standard Bagnouls–Gaussen test. 5–6 dry months is a dry summer; 7 or more is a long, dry summer.
  • Climate uses research-grade records only, inside the species' native range.
  • Summer dormancy flag: species that growers report going dormant in cultivation (Terraforums threads). Anecdotal; some growers keep the same species growing all year.
